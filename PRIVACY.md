# Privacy policy

Bob of The Idle Process runs on your own PC.

This program will not transfer any information to other networked systems unless specifically requested by the user or the person installing or operating it.

- **What Bob collects:** nothing. Bob has no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry.
- **Where your data stays:** chats, notes, settings and downloaded models stay in folders on your PC (see "Where Bob keeps things" in the README). You can delete them at any time, or use Remove Bob in Settings.
- **When Bob goes online:** only when you click. That covers model and engine downloads (from the address shown before you confirm), voice samples from a voice's publisher, web research you ask for, update checks (off unless you turn them on), links you click (they open in your normal web browser), "Search the web" from the right-click menu (opens DuckDuckGo in your browser with the selected words), and cloud AI helpers you switch on with your own API key (each use asks first). What you send to a cloud helper is then covered by that provider's own privacy policy.
- **Phone access** over Tailscale is off unless you turn it on.
- **Hands** (files, running programs, schedules) are off unless you turn them on, and work only on this PC. They send nothing online by themselves; a command you approve can of course use the network (for example `git pull`), and the card shows you the exact command first. The optional "Bob schedules" Windows task only starts Bob on this PC.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/MouseWurX/bob/issues.
